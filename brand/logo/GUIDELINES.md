# BILGE: Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo

**Idea:** an open book whose pages rise into the Ala-Too peaks, with the sun coming up between them. Knowledge is the climb, and the view at the top is your future. Look again and it's also a figure raising both arms: the moment you get in.

| Version | File | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (primary) | `master/bilge-horizontal.svg` | Navbar, website header, documents, email signatures |
| Stacked | `master/bilge-stacked.svg` | Posters, social cards, merch, anything square or tall |
| Symbol | `master/bilge-symbol.svg` | Avatars, loading states, decorative use from 24 px up |
| Symbol, small cut | `master/bilge-symbol-small.svg` | Anything **under 24 px**: favicon, tab icons, inline icons |
| Wordmark | `master/bilge-wordmark.svg` | Where the symbol already appears nearby |
| Reversed | `master/*-reversed.svg` | Dark backgrounds (`#111827`) and Bilge Blue backgrounds |

One-colour files (black, white, Bilge Blue) and PNGs are in `variants/`. Browser and app icons are in `web-icons/`.

## 2. Clear space

Keep the area around the logo empty:
- **Horizontal and stacked lockups:** leave a margin of **1 × the x-height of the wordmark** (the height of the "e") on all sides.
- **Symbol on its own:** leave a margin of **2 × the sun's diameter**.

These margins scale with the logo, so never use a fixed pixel distance.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 24 px | 7 mm |
| Symbol, small cut | 16 px | 5 mm |

Below 24 px, the spine of the regular symbol closes up. Always switch to the small cut at those sizes.

## 4. Colour

| Name | Role | HEX | RGB | CMYK (starting point) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bilge Blue** | Book pages, primary UI accent | `#2563EB` | 37 99 235 | 84 58 0 8 |
| **Kun Amber** | The sun, used only in the logo and for small highlights | `#F59E0B` | 245 158 11 | 0 36 96 4 |
| **Ink** | Wordmark, dark backgrounds | `#111827` | 17 24 39 | 56 38 0 85 |
| White | Reversed logo, backgrounds | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 |

Bilge Blue is the app's existing `--color-primary`, so the logo and UI share one blue.

**Before ordering print:** the CMYK values above are straight conversions. Have the printer match them to a proof. Pantone candidates to check against a physical swatch book are 2728 C / 285 C for Bilge Blue and 137 C for Kun Amber.

**Contrast:**
- Bilge Blue on white: 5.2 : 1. This passes for text and graphics.
- Kun Amber on white: about 2.1 : 1. Use it only for the sun and for decoration, **never for text**.

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- Full colour on white or `gray-50`
- Reversed (white pages, amber sun) on Ink `#111827`
- Reversed on Bilge Blue `#2563EB`
- Black on white
- White on photos (place it on a calm area of the photo)

**Not approved:** the full-colour (blue) logo on black or dark backgrounds. The blue pages disappear. Use the reversed version instead.

## 5. Typography

- **Wordmark:** custom-drawn geometric lowercase. It is not a font, so there is no licence to worry about. Never retype "bilge" in a font to imitate it.
- **UI and body text:** Inter (SIL Open Font License), as already used in the app.

## 6. Don'ts

Don't:
- stretch, squash or rotate the logo
- recolour it outside the palette (for example, a red sun or gradient pages)
- add shadows, outlines, glows or 3D effects
- move the sun or change the gap between the pages
- change the size relationship between symbol and wordmark in a lockup
- place the logo on busy images without a calm area or container
- use the regular symbol below 24 px (use the small cut)

## 7. Master files & source

- All masters are generated by `source/build_kit.py`, so geometry changes happen there and nowhere else.
- **Construction:**
  - Inner page edges are exactly 60°; bottom edges are exactly 15°.
  - The sun sits on the vertical axis, with equal clearance from both inner edges.
  - The wordmark uses a single stroke weight of 26 units. Its round letters overshoot the x-height by 1.5 units.
- The SVGs contain no live text, strokes, filters or rasters (checked with `svg_audit`).

## 8. Handover notes

- **Tests run:**
  - size ladder from 16 to 256 px
  - pixel test at 16, 32 and 48 px on light and dark backgrounds
  - one-colour, greyscale and reversed versions
  - blur ("squint") test
  - mirror and 180° rotation
  - favicon, app icon and header contexts
  - comparison against other education logos
- **Known limit:** the regular symbol loses its spine below 24 px. This is solved by the small cut.
- **Open items:**
  - Trademark search before public launch, at Kyrgyzpatent and the WIPO Global Brand Database. Not done here.
  - Physical Pantone match.
  - Optional: a pattern and icon style built from the peak and sun, for the Universities, Scholarships and ORT sections.
